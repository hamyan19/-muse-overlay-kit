Her first thought was not herself. Her mother had been sitting right beside her. Juliane searched the wreckage around her, calling, listening. ||Nothing.
What she found instead was a bag of sweets, scattered from the wreck. It became her only food. It would be gone in four days.
Now, picture her situation honestly, because this is where most survival stories would end. Seventeen years old. Injured. Half-blind without her glasses. One shoe. A short, sleeveless dress, in a rainforest, in the rainy season.
Above her, in the days that followed, she could sometimes hear the search planes. ||She could hear them. They could never see her. The canopy that had saved her life was now hiding her from rescue.
So she made the decision this entire story turns on. She would not wait to be found.
And she knew exactly what to do, because of something her father had taught her, years before, in this very forest.
